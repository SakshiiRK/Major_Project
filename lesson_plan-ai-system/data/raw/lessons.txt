• How do a plant become tree?
• Are there some plants at your home?
• Why trees are important for our life and health?
• Do you like fruits and dry fruits?
• Which gas do we get from trees?
ANNOUNCEMENT OF TOPIC:

•  The teacher will make the class and students active by saying that the topic which she is
   going to deliver is much more important for all over the world and for all over the human
   beings.
PRESENTATION:

• The teacher will deliver the topic with the help of examples. (such as)
• By giving an example of black board, chairs, tables, furniture, doors, windows, etc.
• By giving an example of paper, pencils, paper board etc.
• The teacher will explain the importance of trees and their role in our life.
• From trees we get the most important gas.
ACTIVITY:

• The teacher will give an activity to the students individually to write down the names of
  the aids which are present in their class room and in their school.
HOMEWORK:

•   The teacher will give some questions to the students for home work.
•   To write down the name of the materials are present in their houses (such as)
•   Door
•   Window
•   Decoration pieces etc.
•   To write an essay about trees and their role in our life.
